# Episode 4: Jorja's Little Big Day

**Audience:** ages 2–5, especially younger toddlers  
**Target runtime:** 3:30–4:00 with two song choruses and response pauses  
**Learning goals:** name familiar objects; identify red, blue, and yellow; sort one soft and one hard object; count one and two.  
**Lead:** Jorja, age two. She is a curious Black toddler who uses short words, gestures, repetition, and delighted reactions. She does not need to answer every question correctly.  
**Supporting characters:** Sunny and Nia; an unseen caregiver remains nearby.  
**Location:** a warm playroom opening onto the Bright Roots garden.  
**Music:** original, gentle 4/4 preschool groove around 94 BPM. Keep the music quiet under speech. Lyrics are original.

## 0:00–0:25 — Hello, Jorja!

**VISUAL:** Jorja toddles into the playroom carrying a soft teddy bear. Sunny and Nia sit at her eye level. Large, uncluttered shapes and objects remain on screen long enough to recognize.

**SUNNY:** Hello, bright learners! Meet our friend Jorja. She's two, and she's ready to explore.

**JORJA:** Hi! Bear!

**NIA:** You found your bear. What do you notice about it?

**JORJA:** Soft! [She gives it a gentle hug.]

**SUNNY:** Yes, a soft bear. Can you say “bear” with Jorja? [Pause 3 seconds.]

## 0:25–1:05 — A red ball

**VISUAL:** A large red ball rests on the rug. Jorja notices it and points. Nia rolls it slowly toward Jorja; Jorja rolls it back. Keep the ball and color visible during the question.

**JORJA:** Ball! Red ball!

**NIA:** That's right—a red ball. It rolls. Ready? Roll it to me!

**SUNNY:** What color is Jorja's ball? [Pause 3 seconds.]

**JORJA and NIA:** Red!

**NIA:** Is the ball soft like your bear? [Jorja squeezes the plush bear, then lightly taps the firm ball.]

**JORJA:** Bear soft. Ball... bouncy!

**SUNNY:** Objects can feel different. Great noticing, Jorja.

## 1:05–1:50 — Look, name, play song

**VISUAL:** Show only the named object on each line. Jorja points rather than performing a complicated dance. Repeat the chorus once for runtime.

**NIA sings:**

Look, look, what do you see?  
A little red ball rolling to me.  
Look, look, what do you know?  
A soft brown bear says hello!

**SUNNY and NIA sing:**

Point and name it, one by one,  
Learning little things is fun.  
Look, touch gently, say what you see—  
Come and learn with Jorja and me!

**JORJA, spoken on the last beat:** Me!

## 1:50–2:25 — Blue cup, yellow flower

**VISUAL:** On a low table sits an empty blue cup. Through the open doorway, a yellow flower grows in a garden pot. Jorja may point to the flower, but does not pick or taste it.

**JORJA:** Cup!

**SUNNY:** A blue cup. Where is the blue cup? [Pause 3 seconds. Jorja points.]

**NIA:** And outside, I see a yellow flower. Can you point to the yellow flower? [Pause 3 seconds.]

**JORJA:** Yellow! Pretty!

**SUNNY:** We can look at the flower with our eyes. A grown-up can help us learn which plants are safe to touch.

## 2:25–3:05 — One, two!

**VISUAL:** Sunny puts one large toy block on the rug, then a second. Both are bigger than a child's mouth and have no detachable small parts. Jorja taps each block as they count.

**SUNNY:** Here's one block. [Pause.] Here comes one more.

**NIA:** How many blocks? Let's touch and count slowly.

**ALL:** One... two!

**JORJA:** Two! Again!

**SUNNY:** One... two! [Pause 2 seconds.] Your turn, bright learners. [Pause 3 seconds.]

**JORJA:** Yay!

## 3:05–3:45 — Jorja's little big review

**VISUAL:** On a clean background, show four objects one at a time: red ball, soft bear, blue cup, yellow flower. End with Jorja waving beside the two blocks.

**NIA:** What rolls? [Pause 3 seconds.]

**JORJA:** Ball!

**SUNNY:** What's soft? [Pause 3 seconds.]

**JORJA:** Bear!

**NIA:** What color is the cup? [Pause 3 seconds.]

**JORJA:** Blue!

**SUNNY:** And how many blocks? [Pause 3 seconds.]

**JORJA:** Two!

**ALL sing:** Big roots, bright futures, grow a little every day. Ask a question, sing a song, let your bright light lead the way!

**SUNNY:** See you next time, Jorja!

**JORJA:** Bye-bye!

## Direction notes

- Jorja is capable and curious. Give her time to observe and initiate words. Her speech is short and clear, without exaggerated baby talk.
- Hold each response pause for the full duration in the final edit. Preschool viewers need time to answer.
- Keep the main objects visually distinct and avoid rapid cuts or flashing effects.
- Use a toddler-safe set: no small loose parts, accessible cords, or unsupervised plant tasting. A caregiver stays close without interrupting the story.
- Record original voices and music before animating mouth movements. The script is a production document, not a finished video.
